Privacy policy

Effective: October 6, 2026 · Applies to the RetroPhone launcher and the RetroPhone Keys keyboard for Android (package com.retrophone.launcher).

The short version. RetroPhone works on your phone. Your apps, notifications, messages, what you type, your searches and your game scores stay on your phone. We collect crash logs and play stats, tied to a random install ID, to improve the app, and you can switch that off in Settings → System → Privacy. We show no ads and sell nothing.

1. Who we are

RetroPhone is developed by LunarSprint ("we", "us"). Contact us about anything in this policy at lunarsprintapps@gmail.com.

2. Data that stays on your phone

RetroPhone needs some information to work as a launcher. It is processed and stored only on your device, is never sent to us, and is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

The "device", model number, serial number and IMEI shown inside RetroPhone are fictional. They are generated randomly, are not your phone's real identifiers, and never leave your phone.

3. Data we collect: crash logs and play stats

We use Firebase Analytics and Firebase Crashlytics, services provided by Google, to understand which features are used and to fix crashes. This is on by default in release versions, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → System → Privacy → Send crash logs + play stats. When it's off, nothing further is collected.

What is collected:

  • Feature events: setup steps completed, OS pack chosen, plans screen viewed, purchase started, games opened, levels and badges reached, secret codes found, fictional system events shown, and what you chose at the coin slot for social apps: insert, continue or walk away (never which app).
  • Coarse app state: current OS pack, phone mode, Era Simulator year, subscription type and level.
  • Crash logs: the technical details of a crash (stack trace, app version, device model, Android version, free memory and disk).
  • Information Firebase collects automatically: an app-instance ID (a random identifier for this installation), device model, Android version, app version, language, approximate country/region derived from your IP address, and session timing.

What is never collected: the names of your apps, your notifications or messages, anything you type, your searches, your contacts, your screen time, your precise location, or your advertising ID. RetroPhone does not request the advertising ID and does not use ad personalisation.

Google processes this data on our behalf under the Firebase data processing terms. See also Privacy and security in Firebase.

4. Purchases

RetroPhone Plus subscriptions are sold and processed by Google Play. We never see your payment details. The app receives only the subscription status from Google Play (which plan, whether it's active) to unlock Plus. Google's handling of payment data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with the service providers named above (Google Firebase, Google Play) to provide the features described, or when required by law.

7. How long we keep data

  • Data on your phone: until you delete it, clear the app's storage or uninstall.
  • Analytics events: kept in Firebase for up to 2 months, then deleted automatically. Aggregated reports that can't identify an installation may be kept longer.
  • Crash reports: kept in Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Emails you send us (including deletion requests): as long as needed to answer them, and no longer than 12 months.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Opt out: Settings → System → Privacy → Share anonymous stats.
  • Delete local data: Android Settings → Apps → RetroPhone → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • Delete collected data: use the data deletion form. Include your Analytics ID (Settings → System → Privacy → Copy ID) so we can find your installation's data. We will delete it within 30 days.
  • Access, correction, objection: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you may have further rights. Email us and we'll respond within 30 days.

9. Children

RetroPhone is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and want their data deleted, use the deletion form.

10. Security

Data sent to Firebase is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Data on your phone is protected by Android's app sandbox.

11. Changes

If we change this policy we'll update the date at the top. Significant changes will also be announced in the app's release notes.
