FAQ

Questions

Does RetroPhone read my notifications or messages?

Only if you allow notification access, and only to show them in the retro panel and control music. They stay on your phone. They are never uploaded, logged or analysed.

Does the keyboard send what I type anywhere?

No. Suggestions are learned on your phone and stored on your phone. The keyboard never sends anything over the internet, and nothing you type is ever collected.

What data do you collect?

Crash logs and play stats, tied to a random install ID, so we can fix bugs and see which packs and games get played. Never your apps, notifications, searches or what you type. You can switch it off in Settings → System → Privacy. Read the privacy policy.

Can I go back to my old launcher?

Any time. Settings → System → Change home app, or pick another launcher in Android's settings. Uninstalling RetroPhone removes everything it stored on your phone. To delete anonymous statistics too, use the data deletion form.

How do I cancel Plus?

In Google Play: Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → RetroPhone Plus. You keep Plus until the end of the period you paid for.
