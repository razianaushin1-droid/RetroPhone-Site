Delete my data

RetroPhone keeps almost everything on your phone. This page explains how to delete it, and lets you ask us to delete the little we collect. You don't need to have the app installed to make a request.

1. Data on your phone

Your settings, pinned apps, keyboard suggestions, game scores, level and badges are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  • Android Settings → Apps → RetroPhone → Storage → Clear storage, or
  • uninstall RetroPhone.

We can't see or delete this data for you, because it never reaches us.

2. Data we collect

Crash logs and play stats (Firebase Crashlytics and Analytics), linked to a random ID for your installation, not to your name or Google account. Without the ID we have no way to find your data, so please include it:

  1. Open RetroPhone and swipe right to Settings.
  2. Open the System tab and scroll to Privacy.
  3. Tap Copy ID (for data deletion) and paste it below.

Already uninstalled? Send the request anyway. Play stats are deleted automatically after 2 months and crash logs after 90 days, and we'll delete any emails we hold from you.

3. Request deletion

What should we delete?

We confirm by email and delete within 30 days. We use your email only to answer this request, and delete it afterwards.

4. Subscriptions and payments

Payments for RetroPhone Plus are handled by Google Play. We never receive your payment details. To cancel, open Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions. Google keeps purchase records under its own privacy policy; you can manage them in your Google Account.

5. Questions

Email lunarsprintapps@gmail.com or read the privacy policy.
